# Workshop record: Wingtip Logistics

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| When is a task at risk? | a task is at risk when its status says so, or when it is due within 7 days and less than 80% complete |
| When is a project at risk? | when at least 35% of its open tasks are at risk |
| When does your financial year start? | October |
| When is a task done? | when the done box is ticked |
| How do you mark a milestone? | with the milestone tick box |
| Is this a regulated business where a person must approve what is written? | no |

Words the customer uses:

- "initiative" means project
